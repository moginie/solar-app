# Solar

A simple Android app that shows live data from a Fronius inverter on your home Wi-Fi:
what the panels are producing, what the house is using, what's going to the grid,
today's energy, and what you've earned from feeding solar into the grid.

## Install

1. On your Android phone, download **[Solar-android.apk](https://github.com/moginie/solar-app/releases/latest/download/Solar-android.apk)**.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser (or Files app) to **install unknown apps**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open **Solar**.

The phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the inverter. The app finds the
inverter automatically; if it can't, open Settings (⚙) and tap **Find inverter on this Wi-Fi**.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
